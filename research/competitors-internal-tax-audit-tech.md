# Competitors: internal tax audit (pre-audit) for tech companies in India

*Date: 14 Oct 2026. Product being compared: a company-side, AI-driven **internal tax audit before the external audit**, built for GCCs, IT/ITES and SaaS companies. It covers GST (export refunds, overseas-branch transactions, cross-charge, reverse charge), TDS including Sec 195 / 15CA-CB, transfer-pricing / intercompany checks, 3CD readiness, a notice tracker and an audit-ready pack.*

**Caveats:**
- Most vendor details come from vendor or directory pages and were **not independently verified**.
- Items marked † are from general knowledge.
- **No single competitor was found that does the whole job.** Each one covers a slice.

## Where the white space is

No competitor found combines all of the following:
1. **Company-side, continuous** pre-audit (not CA-side year-end, not reactive notice tracking)
2. **Tech-industry tax logic**: GST export refunds, overseas-branch transactions, the 57th GST Council changes, cross-charge/ISD, 195/15CA-CB, TP intercompany checks, PE flags
3. **Internal-audit-grade output**: working papers, a risk-rated findings report, and an audit-ready pack for the external CA

## Threat ranking (judgement)

| Rank | Competitor | Why it's a threat | Defence |
|------|-----------|-------------------|---------|
| 1 | **Clear** | Data, enterprise relationships, already framing "tax assurance" | Stay independent (assurance, not filing). Go deep on tech-specific TP and 195. Integrate with Clear rather than replace it. |
| 2 | **Big 4 / BDO / RSM** | Own the GCC relationships and bundle services | Partner as their delivery tool, or win on price and continuity |
| 3 | **Masters India / IRIS (Sovos)** | Enterprise GST plus notice and audit modules | Broader tax scope and audit-grade outputs |
| 4 | Notice and litigation tools | Same buyer (tax head) | Prevention plus root-cause linkage |

**Next steps:** demo 3–4 of these tools (Clear, Masters India, IRIS, Litigation Shift) and ask 5–10 GCC or IT tax heads what they use today and what is missing.
