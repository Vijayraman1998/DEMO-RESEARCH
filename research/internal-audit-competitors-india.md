# Internal audit software in India: competitor map

*Research date: 14 Oct 2026. This is a companion to `internal-audit-large-companies-india.md`. Most vendor details come from vendor pages or review sites and have **not** been independently verified. Items marked † are from general knowledge, not from a source found in this research.*

## Summary

The market is split into five groups:

1. **Global GRC and audit platforms.** Expensive and built for Fortune 500 companies.
2. **Indian audit-management tools.** Mostly workflow software with a BFSI (banking and financial services) tilt.
3. **Compliance and regulatory-tracking tools.** Adjacent to internal audit.
4. **Tax-compliance tools.** They do GST/TDS filing and reconciliation, but not audit.
5. **The real competitor for budget: audit firms.** The Big 4, mid-tier firms and CA firms, using Excel, IDEA or ACL.

**Gap:** No one clearly owns *continuous, full-population audit of finance and tax controls* for Indian large companies. That means data pulled from ERP, GSTN and TDS sources, with audit-trail checks and working papers produced automatically.

| **IRIS GST, Cygnet, Avalara India** † | Enterprise GST and e-invoicing compliance |

These tools *file and reconcile* taxes. They do not provide independent audit assurance, working papers or audit-committee reporting. That leaves room to partner with them or position against them.

## 5. Services firms (the main competitor for internal audit budget)

- **Big 4 India:** Deloitte, EY, PwC, KPMG. Combined India revenue is about ₹38,800 cr (FY24). They bring their own platforms: Deloitte Omnia/Argus, PwC Aura/Halo, EY Canvas/Helix, KPMG Clara/Ignite. [Shanlax journal](https://www.shanlaxjournals.in/journals/index.php/sijash/article/download/10640/9034/), [The Finance Story](https://thefinancestory.com/big-4-india-ey-deloitte-pwc-kpmg-eyes-revenue-₹45000-cr)
- **Mid-tier firms** †: Grant Thornton Bharat, BDO India, Protiviti India, plus many CA firms such as PKC.
- **Usual toolset:** IDEA, ACL, Power BI, Tableau and Excel. [Shanlax journal](https://www.shanlaxjournals.in/journals/index.php/sijash/article/download/10640/9034/)

**Go-to-market implication:** a new product could be sold *to* these firms as their delivery tool (co-sourcing), not only *against* them.
