# TAM, SAM and SOM for the full platform (all components built)

*Date: 15 Oct 2026. Question answered: once the complete component set in `product-components-audit-ready.md` is built (tax audit + full internal audit + IFC testing + compliance calendar + notice/litigation tracking + audit-ready handoff), what is the market?*

**Health warning.** No published report sizes this niche. Everything below is a **bottom-up estimate** built on assumed entity counts and assumed spend per entity. The end-state figures compound several assumptions, so the uncertainty band is wide (roughly ±40%). Treat them as a direction of travel, not a forecast. Inputs that come from sources are cited; everything else is an assumption you can change.

---

## Why the numbers move when the full component set is built

Building the whole workflow lets you charge against **four budget lines instead of one**:

| Budget line | Who owns it | Unlocked by component |
|-------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Internal tax audit / tax review | Head of Tax | 5 (tax modules) |
| Internal audit | Chief Audit Executive | 2, 4, 6, 7 |
| IFC / ICFR testing and external-audit prep | CFO / Controller | 4, 7, 9 |
| Compliance tracking and notice/litigation management | Tax and compliance teams | 8, 10 |

Average contract value therefore roughly **doubles** (about ₹15 lakh → about ₹30 lakh for a mid-to-large GCC), and the buyer set widens from one person to three.

## Stage 3: full platform, all sectors (end state)

Extends the full component set beyond tech to every Indian entity that must run internal audit: **~40,000 corporates** under Sec 138, plus banks, **~650 NBFCs/HFCs** under RBI's risk-based internal audit mandate ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/economy-policy/rbi-makes-risk-based-internal-audit-mandatory-for-select-nbfcs-ucbs-121020302071_1.html)), cooperative banks and insurers.

### TAM ≈ ₹41,500 cr spend / ₹7,450 cr software

| Layer | Spend TAM | Software TAM | Source of the estimate |
|-------|-----------|--------------|------------------------|
| Internal audit (all entities) | ₹28,300 cr | ₹4,900 cr | `internal-audit-tam-sam.md` |
| Internal tax audit / tax review | ₹9,500 cr | ₹1,850 cr | 5,000 × ₹1 cr + 10,000 × ₹25 L + 25,000 × ₹8 L (assumption) |
| Compliance tracking + notice/litigation | ₹4,000 cr | ₹700 cr | 40,000 × about ₹10 L average (assumption) |
| **Total** | **≈ ₹41,800 cr (~$4.7B)** | **≈ ₹7,450 cr (~$850M)** | |

**Cross-checks on the software figure:**
- India's eGRC software market was **$458.5M in 2024, forecast to reach $930M by 2029** ([MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/geography/enterprise-governance-risk-compliance-market/India)). Our ₹7,450 cr ≈ $850M sits at that 2029 forecast. eGRC excludes tax-compliance software and includes cyber GRC we would not do, so the comparison is directional, not exact.
- Global internal audit services are about **$74.8B** ([Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/report/internal-audit)). India at 3–4% is $2.2–3B, against our ₹28,300 cr ≈ $3.2B internal audit layer. Our figure is at the top of that range.

## Sensitivity

| Variable | Change | Effect on Stage 2 SAM |
|----------|--------|----------------------|
| Full-platform ACV for mid-to-large GCCs | ₹20 L instead of ₹30 L | ₹500 cr (−24%) |
| Indian legal entities per GCC parent | 1.5 instead of 1.0 | ₹880 cr (+34%) |
| Mid Indian IT/ITES count | 300 or 800 instead of 500 | ₹610 cr or ₹730 cr |
| Only 2 of the 4 budget lines win | Tax + internal audit only | ≈ ₹450 cr |

**The biggest single assumption is that one platform can win all four budget lines.** In practice tax and internal audit often sit in different reporting lines, and compliance tracking may already be with an incumbent (TeamLease RegTech, Komrisk). If the product only ever wins the tax and internal audit budgets, Stage 2 SAM is about **₹450 cr**, not ₹650 cr.

## What to verify before using these numbers externally

1. **Entity counts by revenue band.** No official source was found for Indian IT/ITES or for corporates by turnover. Check MCA21 (AOC-4), CMIE Prowess, or the NASSCOM member directory.
2. **Whether GCC parents run more than one Indian legal entity.** This single variable swings SAM by a third.
3. **ACV.** Test in paid pilots. Indian buyers are price-sensitive and the ₹30 lakh full-platform figure is untested.
4. **Budget-line consolidation.** Ask in discovery calls whether one buyer can authorise tax, internal audit and compliance spend together.
