# TAM, SAM and SOM for internal tax audit (pre-audit) in the tech industry, India

*Date: 14 Oct 2026. Replaces the sizing in section 4 of `tax-audit-tech-industry.md`.*

**Product:** company-side, AI-driven internal tax audit run **before** the external statutory and Sec 44AB tax audit. It covers GST (export refunds, HO–BO, cross-charge, reverse charge), TDS including Sec 195, transfer-pricing / intercompany checks, Form 3CD readiness, a notice tracker and an audit-ready pack.

**Method:** no published report sizes this niche, so the sizing uses **three methods that check each other**:
1. Current spend on this kind of work
2. Software contract value
3. Value at stake

Inputs from sources are cited. Everything else is an **assumption** and is labelled so you can change it.

---

## Summary

| Layer | Estimate | Meaning |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **TAM: current spend** | **≈ ₹1,300 cr / yr (~$150M)** | What tech companies in India spend today on internal tax-review work: in-house time, tax health checks, GST/TDS reviews, 3CD pre-audit preparation and intercompany reconciliation |

| **SAM** | **≈ ₹325 cr / yr (~$37M)** | Segments reachable in 3–5 years: mid-size GCCs, IT/ITES ₹200–2,000 cr, and larger SaaS |

| **SOM (about 3 years)** | **≈ ₹9–13 cr ARR (~$1–1.5M)** | 70–100 customers, about 3–4% of SAM |

The SAM is growing at about **10–12% a year**. GCCs grew from about 1,700 in FY24 to 2,117 in FY26.

The ₹ to $ conversion uses about ₹88 per $1.

## 2. TAM method 1: current spend on internal tax-review work ≈ ₹1,300 cr

**Included:** in-house tax-team time on review and reconciliation; external tax health checks; GST and TDS reviews; 3CD pre-audit preparation; intercompany / TP data reconciliation (not the TP study itself).

**Excluded:** statutory audit fees, filing software, litigation fees.

| Segment | Entities | Annual spend each (assumption) | Total |
|---------|----------|-------------------------------|-------|
| A + D: Mega GCCs and large IT | ~190 | ₹1.5 cr | ₹285 cr |
| C: Other GCCs | 1,530 | ₹40 lakh | ₹612 cr |
| B: Mid-market GCCs | 480 | ₹15 lakh | ₹72 cr |
| E: Mid IT/ITES | 500 | ₹40 lakh | ₹200 cr |
| F: Small IT/ITES | 1,000 | ₹10 lakh | ₹100 cr |
| G: SaaS | 450 | ₹12 lakh | ₹54 cr |
| **TAM (spend)** | **~4,150** | | **≈ ₹1,323 cr (~$150M)** |

**Benchmarks for the spend assumptions:**
- ICAI-recommended *minimum* fees are about ₹37,500+ for a tax audit and ₹22,500+ for a TDS review ([ICAI fee schedule](https://upnrhm.gov.in/assets/site-files/tenders/new_document/Minimum%20fee%20recomended%20by%20ICAI.pdf)). Real fees for GCCs and mid-size IT companies are many times higher.
- The non-compliance penalty for TP documentation is 2% of the transaction value ([Bajaj Finserv](https://bajajfinserv.in/investments/transfer-pricing-audit)).
- A mid-size GCC typically has 2–5 tax/finance staff spending part of the year on review work, plus one or more external reviews (judgement).

## 4. TAM method 3: value at stake (cross-check)

- ClearTax reports about **₹3 cr of ITC left unclaimed per enterprise per year**, and **80% of GST notices are avoidable** ([Clear](https://www.cleartax.com/in/india-cfo-survey-report), vendor survey).
- 4,150 entities × ₹3 cr gives about **₹12,000 cr per year of recoverable value**, before counting notices, interest, disallowances and refund delays.
- A software price of 3–4% of the value recovered gives **₹370–500 cr**, which **agrees with Method 2**.

## 5. SAM ≈ ₹325 cr

These are the segments a startup can realistically win in 3–5 years with a remote, software-only product.

| Included | Why | Entities | ACV | SAM |
|----------|-----|----------|-----|-----|
| C: Other GCCs (mid to large) | Complex tax (TP, GST export, 195, PE), budget, fully digital, India finance teams can buy | 1,530 | ₹15 lakh | ₹230 cr |
| B: Mid-market GCCs | Same pain, smaller teams, fewer advisers | 480 | ₹6 lakh | ₹29 cr |
| E: Mid IT/ITES (₹200–2,000 cr) | Export refunds, overseas branches, cross-charge. Internal audit is mandatory. | 500 | ₹12 lakh | ₹60 cr |
| G: SaaS with revenue > $10M | Indian entity of a US parent: TP, 195, GST export | 150 | ₹5 lakh | ₹7.5 cr |
| **SAM** | | **~2,660** | | **≈ ₹326 cr (~$37M)** |

**Left out of SAM, and why:**

| Segment | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| A + D: Mega GCCs and top ~100 IT | Global tax tools from the parent (ONESOURCE/Vertex), Big 4 managed services, long procurement. Phase 2. |
| F: Small IT/ITES and small SaaS | Low budget. Better served later through CA firms or self-serve. |

**Expansion SAM:** adding full internal audit (payroll, P2P, revenue/contracts, ITGC) for segments C and E at about ₹10 lakh more ACV adds ~2,030 × ₹10 lakh ≈ ₹200 cr. That brings it to **≈ ₹530 cr**.

## 7. Sensitivity and caveats

| Variable | Change | Effect |
|----------|--------|--------|
| ACV for segment C | ₹10 lakh instead of ₹15 lakh | SAM about ₹250 cr |
| Indian legal entities per GCC | 1.5 instead of 1.0 | SAM about ₹440 cr |
| Mid IT count (E) | 300–800 instead of 500 | SAM about ₹300–360 cr |

**Biggest uncertainties:**
- Number of Indian IT/ITES companies by revenue band. No source was found. Check MCA, CMIE Prowess or the NASSCOM directory.
- Contract values. Test them in paid pilots.
- Tax-review spend per company. Ask in the first 20 discovery calls.

**Context:** this niche is small (SAM ≈ ₹325 cr) compared with the full internal audit SAM (≈ ₹2,600 cr, see `internal-audit-tam-sam.md`). It works as an entry point, not the end-state market. The plan is to expand into full internal audit in tech, then into pharma and auto.
