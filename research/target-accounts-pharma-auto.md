# Target accounts: pharma / medical devices and auto components (India)

*Date: 14 Oct 2026. Companies chosen to fit the SAM sweet spot (about ₹500–5,000 cr revenue, listed so internal audit is mandatory, multi-plant).*

**Important:** company names, HQ cities and revenue bands come from general knowledge (roughly FY24/FY25) and were **not re-verified for this list**. Before outreach:
- Check each company's latest revenue on Screener.in.
- Check who runs its internal audit (in-house or outsourced): the annual report's corporate governance section names the internal auditor.
- Find the current CFO, Head of Internal Audit and Indirect Tax Head on LinkedIn or in the annual report.

Very large players (Sun, Cipla, Dr Reddy's, Lupin, Bosch, Uno Minda, Motherson, Bharat Forge and others) are left out on purpose. They are locked into SAP GRC and Big 4 platforms.

## C. Who to contact at each company (roles, not names)

| Priority | Role | Opening message |
|----------|------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Head of Internal Audit / Chief Audit Executive** | Full-population testing of P2P, O2C and inventory without adding headcount |
| 2 | **CFO** | Fewer audit observations and notices, lower internal audit cost, a ready Audit Committee deck |
| 3 | **Head of Indirect Tax / GST** | ITC at risk, GSTR mismatches |
| 4 | **Partner at the company's outsourced internal audit firm** (named in the annual report) | Channel: use the tool across all their clients |

**Suggested first 20 calls** (city-clustered for fast references; the pharma names are a cluster, not a ranking):

- **8 pharma companies:** for example Granules, Natco, Neuland (Hyderabad), plus Indoco, Marksans, FDC, Unichem, JB Chem (Mumbai).
- **8 auto component companies:** for example Lumax Industries, Subros, Sandhar, Jamna, Fiem, Shriram Pistons (NCR), plus Pricol and Sansera (South).
- **4 internal audit firms:** the ones named in these companies' annual reports.
