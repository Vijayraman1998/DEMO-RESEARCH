# What users actually say: reviews and complaints about internal audit / control testing tools

*Date: 15 Oct 2026. Source type: public software reviews (G2, Capterra, PeerSpot, TrustRadius, Gartner Peer Insights), vendor-neutral write-ups, and Protiviti's SOX practitioner poll.*

## What this research is and is not

**What I found:** reviews written by **internal audit, SOX and controls practitioners** — many of them at tech and software companies — about the tools they use today. These are the closest thing to direct customer voice available publicly.

**What I could not get:**
- **Reddit is blocked to this tool**, so r/Accounting and r/internalaudit threads are not covered.
- **No India- or GCC-specific practitioner complaints** were found. Searches on GCC controllership and SOX returned only consultancy marketing. This is a real gap: the evidence below is mostly US/global practitioner voice. **Assume it transfers to Indian GCCs, but verify in discovery calls.**
- Review samples are **small and span several years**, and some mix legacy and current product versions. Treat individual quotes as signals, not statistics.

---

## Theme 1: the tools are slow, and people lose work

The single most repeated complaint across every major vendor.

| What users said | Product |
|-----------------|---------|
| "some functions such as scoping can take up to **five minutes to load**", and "workpapers sometimes **fail to save or freeze**, risking lost work" | MetricStream ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/audit-management-solutions/vendor/metricstream)) |
| Bugs, update-related glitches and slowdowns that disrupt workflow; lag "particularly when editing documents or handling large files" | AuditBoard ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/148230/SOXHUB/reviews/), [Sprinto](https://sprinto.com/blog/auditboard-review/)) |
| "somewhat slow, particularly when moving between different sections or when opening larger files" | TeamMate+ ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/teammate/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons)) |
| Slow loading times among the top aggregated complaint tags | Workiva ([G2](https://g2.com/products/workiva-workiva/reviews?page=5)) |
| "application sometimes hangs during peak hours" | eTHIC ([SoftwareAdvice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/financial-risk-management/ethic-profile/reviews/)) |
| Report generation slow on large data volumes | Ideagen ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/audit-management-solutions/vendor/ideagen)) |

**Implication:** performance is a real differentiator, not a hygiene factor. Target sub-second page loads and never lose a working paper. Make it a benchmarked claim in sales.

## Theme 2: implementation takes far longer than promised

| What users said | Product |
|-----------------|---------|
| "the setup for SOX Compliance can be **very tiresome**"; initial setup "took **far longer than expected** when done in-house" | Workiva ([G2](https://g2.com/products/workiva-workiva/reviews?page=2)) |
| "**long implementation cycles, steep learning curves**, and a total cost of ownership that climbs fast" | MetricStream ([Sprinto](https://sprinto.com/blog/metricstream-review/)) |
| Complexity of implementation and a time-consuming setup process; "steep learning curve"; one reviewer found it hard to use without formal training | AuditBoard ([Sprinto](https://sprinto.com/blog/auditboard-review/), [B2B Reviews](https://www.b2breviews.com/reviews/auditboard/), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/148230/SOXHUB/reviews/)) |
| "not very intuitive"; "a learning curve for some of the new users on our team" | TeamMate+ ([Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/143239/TeamMate-Audit-Management/reviews/)) |

**Implication:** the strongest possible wedge claim is **"first control tested in week one"**. Pre-built control libraries and ERP connectors that work out of the box, not a six-month consulting project.

## Theme 3: no content out of the box, so customers build it themselves

| What users said | Product |
|-----------------|---------|
| "MetricStream at that point did not have a template, and **I had to build the entire SOX 404 IT general controls testing framework myself**" | MetricStream ([PeerSpot](https://peerspot.com/products/metricstream-pros-and-cons)) |
| "doesn't provide a good option for **cloning existing controls**" | AuditBoard ([PeerSpot](https://www.peerspot.com/products/auditboard-pros-and-cons)) |
| "Analytics and Governance are **to be implemented**" | eTHIC ([SoftwareAdvice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/financial-risk-management/ethic-profile/reviews/)) |

**Implication:** ship a **ready-made control and test library** (ITGC, SoD, R2R, revenue, payroll), not an empty framework. This is where an AI-first product can beat incumbents outright: generate the risk-control matrix from the customer's own ERP configuration and process documents.

## Theme 4: analytics, sampling and Excel handling are weak

| What users said | Product |
|-----------------|---------|
| The "sampling tool **falls short**"; finicky attachments; difficulty with large or password-protected files; workflow capabilities "lacking compared to Excel or Word" | Workiva ([G2](https://g2.com/products/workiva-workiva/reviews?page=4), [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/servicenow-servicenow-integrated-risk-management-vs-workiva-workiva)) |
| "annotation doesn't allow for excel files to be annotated unless you do PDF conversion"; survey analysis weak; "some modules can't manage a lot of data" | AuditBoard ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/148230/SOXHUB/reviews/), [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/auditboard/reviews)) |
| "limited analytics features" listed as a con | Audit360 ([Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/1094543/Audit360)) |

**Implication:** this is the clearest opening. Incumbents are **workflow and documentation tools with sampling bolted on**. A product that tests the **full population** from ERP data, rather than pulling a sample of 25, is a category difference, not a feature difference.

## Theme 5: the remediation loop is broken

> "issues have to be **tracked manually** rather than flowing from control through remediation" — Workiva ([G2](https://g2.com/products/workiva-workiva/reviews?page=4))

**Implication:** close the loop in the product: failed control → deficiency → owner → remediation → **automatic re-test** → evidence of closure. This is also what stops findings repeating year after year.

## Theme 6: rigidity, navigation and admin friction

| What users said | Product |
|-----------------|---------|
| "more limiting in creating **ad hoc audit processes**" than Archer and TeamMate+ | AuditBoard ([PeerSpot](https://www.peerspot.com/products/auditboard-pros-and-cons)) |
| "user roles and groups can be **very confusing**"; could not access "more than 15 items in one page" when batch editing; problems linking and opening documents; limited PowerPoint output | TeamMate+ ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/teammate/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons), [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/143239/TeamMate-Audit-Management/reviews/)) |
| Same information reachable "through too many paths"; hard to train non-experts | SAP audit ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/audit-management-solutions/vendor/sap)) |
| "upgrades require **re-implementing customizations**" after significant upgrades | MetricStream ([PeerSpot](https://peerspot.com/products/metricstream-pros-and-cons)) |
| Requests for user-configurable MIS reports and audit workflow; dashboards missing in the version used | eTHIC ([SoftwareAdvice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/financial-risk-management/ethic-profile/reviews/)) |

**Implication:** configuration must survive upgrades (config-as-data, not custom code), and bulk operations must have no arbitrary limits.

## Theme 7: cost, and opaque pricing

- Workiva: cost called "the main downside"; pricing "steep for a company of their size"; no free tier or trial ([G2](https://g2.com/products/workiva-workiva/reviews?page=5), [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/answers/what-are-the-best-tools-for-sarbanes-oxley-act-compliance))
- MetricStream: "very costly"; "other tools meet the feature requirements and are more cost-effective"; steep price point a drawback for smaller businesses ([PeerSpot](https://peerspot.com/products/metricstream-pros-and-cons), [Digital Project Manager](https://thedigitalprojectmanager.com/tools/metricstream-review/))
- AuditBoard: pricing rated low and "not transparent"; cost grows as modules are added ([B2B Reviews](https://www.b2breviews.com/reviews/auditboard/))
- Archer: the main dislike was "cost complexity" ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/audit-management-solutions/vendor/archer))

**Implication:** this directly validates the India mid-market and GCC positioning. **Publish transparent pricing** — it would be a differentiator on its own in this category.

## Theme 8: support quality is inconsistent

- AuditBoard: "a lot of turnover with customer support" ([TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/auditboard/reviews))
- MetricStream: one reviewer rated support about **1 out of 10**, while another praised support and scalability "compared with other products in India" ([PeerSpot](https://peerspot.com/products/metricstream-pros-and-cons))

## The underlying work pain (not tool-specific)

| Finding | Source |
|---------|--------|
| Staff spend time "chasing down spreadsheets, emails, and screenshots", manually organising documentation, and managing inconsistent file formats and version histories | [Safebooks](https://safebooks.ai/resources/sox-compliance/sox-testing-the-complete-guide/) (vendor) |
| "a folder of spreadsheets doesn't help if it doesn't show the control was performed, reviewed, and approved". Missing review evidence is a common finding: "the control was performed, but nothing shows someone reviewed it" | [Trullion](https://trullion.com/blog/sox-audit-guide/) (vendor) |
| Inquiry alone is not adequate testing for SOX 404, which is why auditors keep demanding system evidence | [SEC comment file](https://www.sec.gov/comments/s7-24-06/cklumper2516.pdf) |
| A SOX analyst's job is described as being "the point of contact between IT, Finance, and Internal Audit to keep evidence gathering and submission smooth" — a full-time role created by coordination friction | [Box job posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/boxinc/jobs/6477957) |

## What this means for the product

| Ranked opening | Evidence behind it |
|----------------|--------------------|
| **1. Full-population testing instead of sampling** | Sampling tools "fall short" (Workiva); weak analytics (AuditBoard, Audit360, eTHIC); only 1 in 5 confident in analytics skills (Protiviti) |
| **2. Ready-made control library, live in week one** | A user built the whole SOX 404 ITGC framework himself (MetricStream); no control cloning (AuditBoard); long implementations everywhere |
| **3. Automated evidence collection from source systems** | Chasing spreadsheets, emails and screenshots; inquiry is not adequate testing; a full-time role exists just to coordinate evidence |
| **4. Closed remediation loop with automatic re-testing** | Issues "tracked manually rather than flowing from control through remediation" (Workiva) |
| **5. Lead with ITGC and segregation of duties** | The area where deficiencies most often occur (Protiviti) — and it is pure system data, so no physical audit, which fits the stated constraint |
| **6. Speed, and transparent pricing** | Slowness is the most repeated complaint across all vendors; pricing is opaque or "steep" at all four majors |

## Verify in discovery calls

1. Which tool do you use, and what do you most want to throw out of it?
2. How long did implementation actually take versus what was promised?
3. How many hours go into one control-testing cycle? Does it fit in the window?
4. How much of your evidence is still screenshots and email?
5. How many findings repeat from last year?
6. Where do your deficiencies cluster — is it IT access, as Protiviti's data suggests?
7. What do you pay a year, all in?
